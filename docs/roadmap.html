1 · How to read this

Two roadmap views exist, and they answer different questions:

028 build-order view
Generated (spectastic order, do not hand-edit). Ranks the committed spec corpus so nothing precedes a spec it depends on. It knows only about specs — it cannot show deferred ideas that have no spec yet.
This page
Hand-maintained. Tracks the considerations-doc backlog — the TBD-* moves each research doc recommended, checked off as they land. When a TBD graduates into a spec, it moves into the 028 view.

Ordering here is RICE, lifted from the roadmap-ranking portfolio synthesis. RICE is a value estimate, not a dependency graph — a lower-scored item may still need to precede a higher-scored one (e.g. the artifact-security spec precedes its own CI check). Read the score as "how much this is worth", not "build this first regardless".

The scoring notes worth recording:

  • Capability-accountability principle (16.8 — since shipped as P-12). A one-paragraph principles amendment on the proven P-8/P-9/P-11 rails — cheap (E2), high-confidence (.8) — and the dependency root of the four-item change-risk cluster. Like the July cohort's catalog/security foundations, the cheapest item was also the enabler, so RICE and the precedence graph pointed the same way: it topped the scoring pass and was ratified next, a principle before its teeth (the change-risk scan, TBD-change-risk-score, now unblocked).
  • The cheap-vs-teeth split. The two principle/CLI-config items (catalog-next 12.8, validate --strict 8.4) and the glob-widen docs scan (8.0) clear the field on low effort. The teeth they imply — the change-risk scan (3.7, since shipped as 049), the coverage diff-gate (4.2), the two provenance gates (1.8–2.2) — carry real effort and, for the supply-chain moves, genuine grounding uncertainty (the research doc itself calls obfuscated-backdoor detection undecidable), so they sink. This is the same RICE tension the portfolio pass flagged, not a new finding.
  • Architecture fitness function (1.0). Unchanged from the synthesis and no longer the "next" pick — it only wore that marker because it was the sole scored item in a field of blanks. Its low score is honest: prose won't hold a dependency rule and a low-false-positive check is hard (C.3, E9). Revisit when one exists.
  • Contributor trust (0.3). Deliberately near-zero — the doc records it as unsolved (C.15) with no reliable tooling. It sits at the foot as a ceiling marker, not a queued task.
