Design · 118-verdict-explain

Reviewer-grade verdict explanation

How we will build it.

Status Accepted Spec 118-verdict-explain Owner Brian Corbin · @briancorbinxyz Branch main Created 13 Sep 2026 Read time

Extend the decision parse to carry the decision's title + prose; add a pure explainViolations that joins each violation to its offending snippet, its decision (reason + Context/Decision/Consequences + coordinate), and its sanctioned location (the rule's allowed-in); render it in the triage-card shape (Offending / Why / Sanctioned path) behind an opt-in verdict --explain. Biggest risk: re-typing guidance into the tool — avoided by pulling every word from the decision record, never authoring it here.

6 · Approach

Extend parseDecisions to capture each decision's <h4> title and following prose. Add explainViolations: for each violation, take the offending snippet from the pre-read file content (± a few lines, the line marked), look up the decision by {specId, id} for its title/reason/prose/coordinate, and the rule by id for its allowed-in (else the decision scope). renderExplanations prints the triage-shape blocks. Add --explain to the verdict CLI, output-only, after the terse list; it composes with --teach.

8 · Decisions

D-001 · Compose the explanation from the decision record + the snippet, in the triage shape

Context. The triage card sets the clarity floor (grounding row 1); the verdict already has the data (row 2). Decision. A pure explainViolations + renderExplanations laying out Offending / Why / Sanctioned path. Consequences. + Triage-grade clarity, one source of truth. − A second renderer beside the terse one.

D-002 · Extend the decision parse to carry title + prose

Context. The verdict only extracts the one-line reason (row 3). Decision. Capture the <h4> title and the following prose as title/prose — additive. Consequences. + The explanation shows Context/Decision/Consequences. − Slightly more parse; no consumer breaks (optional fields).

D-003 · Opt-in --explain, output-only

Context. CI wants terse; the reviewer wants rich; the verdict CLI already has --teach. Decision. A --explain flag prints the explanation after the verdict, composing with --teach; the exit code and artifact are untouched. Consequences. + Non-breaking, off the gate. − Rich-by-default is deferred.

D-004 · Sanctioned path = allowed-in / scope, not a named exemplar

Context. Naming the one right file needs the decision to declare it. Decision. Show the rule's allowed-in (else the decision scope) — where the pattern is permitted — plus the decision's own guidance. Consequences. + Honest, from existing data. − Not a click-through to an exemplar (deferred, TBD-verdict-explain-exemplar).

D-005 · Test-first, low risk

Context. A parse extension + a pure renderer. Decision. TDD; low risk. Consequences. + Guarded. − None.

10 · Risks & mitigations

Guidance re-typed into the tool, drifting from the decision

Likelihood low, impact medium. Mitigation: every word of the explanation is pulled from the decision record; the renderer authors none of it (the alternatives matrix records why).

A missing file crashes the explanation

Likelihood medium, impact low. Mitigation: an unreadable snippet renders a plain "file not on disk" note; the Why and Sanctioned-path sections still render (tested).
