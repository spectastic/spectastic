CI recipes landed in 121 — narrow the deferral to the flag

Applied

1 · Intent

031 deferred two things under one slug. One of them has landed: 121-init-ci-gate makes the CI gate a third half of init --tools, marker-managed and drift-checked, running validate, enforce and the merge verdict on GitHub Actions and GitLab CI. Left as written, the item keeps reading as fully pending to the backlog audit and to anyone scanning this spec, and it silently over-states what TBD-validate-strict still owes.

The honest record is a split: one item pointing at the spec that landed the recipes, one narrowed to the standalone --strict flag — which is still deferred, and which 121 itself lists as out of scope under the same slug.

2 · Scope

In scope

  • Split the TBD-validate-strict out-of-scope item into two: the CI-recipe half redirected to 121-init-ci-gate; the --strict flag half kept under TBD-validate-strict, reworded to name only the flag.

Out of scope

  • The --strict flag itself — still deferred, unchanged. defer-to TBD-validate-strict
  • Any change to what 121 installs — owned there. defer-to 121-init-ci-gate

3 · Approach

A data/content delta on the out-of-scope block (REQ-CHANGE-002/008): the target is not requirement-shaped, the delta embeds a <spec-diff>, and apply leaves the requirements body untouched — the §6 task edits the <li>. Two <li>s rather than one reworded one because a defer-to names a single destination and the backlog audit counts per slug: the split keeps TBD-validate-strict open for exactly the flag.

4 · Deltas

One data/content delta. The target names the out-of-scope item by its current slug.

What changes: the single <li> becomes two — the CI-recipe half redirected to the spec that landed it, the flag half narrowed.

<li defer-to="TBD-validate-strict">A general spectastic validate --strict flag + CI recipes for pipeline use (GitHub Actions, etc.). This spec applies the errors + Accepted-open-question rejection at the pre-commit gate; the standalone strict flag for non-hook CI is the sibling.</li> <li defer-to="121-init-ci-gate">CI recipes for pipeline use — landed: 121 makes a marker-managed, drift-guarded CI gate (GitHub Actions + GitLab CI, running validate + enforce + verdict) a third half of init --tools, and regenerates docs/ci-examples/ from the same renderer.</li> <li defer-to="TBD-validate-strict">A general spectastic validate --strict flag. This spec applies the errors + Accepted-open-question rejection at the pre-commit gate; the standalone strict flag for non-hook CI is the sibling, and stays deferred (121 lists it under the same slug).</li>
