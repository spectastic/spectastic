1 · Execution strategy

Setup → Foundational → US1 → US2 → Polish. Each story closes its conformance before the next opens. The right shape here: US2 is a check over what US1 authors, so running them together would mean writing the gate before the thing it gates.

Implement US1 end-to-end and ship it. A design that declares its surface is useful on its own; the gate that keeps a CLI project from being asked is a correctness fix on top.

Not applicable at this size. The foundational reader and detector are shared by both stories, so there is no seam to split a second person onto.
