Seed the observability + SLO principles at verified/enterprise

Applied

1 · Intent

The observability survey makes the case that a project operating in production should make its health observable — the golden signals emitted through a standard registry/exporter — rather than inferred from logs; and that at the highest tier, reliability should be governed by SLOs and error budgets, not opinion. These map cleanly onto the profile ladder: observability is a verified-tier "operate it in production" concern (a team product can start with logs and add it when it ships to prod), and SLOs are an enterprise concern that sits on the verified observability substrate (an SLO needs its SLI and instrumentation first).

These two rows were part of the shape-of-system catalog's first draft; that proposal's adversarial pass argued (correctly) that folding a second survey's topic into a §6-catalog change eroded single-topic reviewability, so they were split here to land under their own topic. They stay principles — advisory, per P-8 — and their enforcement and verify-page proof legs follow as separate specs (see §2). This proposal is deliberately small and single-topic.

2 · Scope

In scope

  • ADD structured observability to the verified preset in spectastic-profiles.json, repeated into the enterprise preset (cumulative-by-repetition; the drift-guard test from the catalog change keeps the two copies identical).
  • ADD service-level objectives to the enterprise preset only.
  • Rebuild the bundle so packages/cli/_bundled/spectastic-profiles.json matches; keep the profile/compose + drift-guard tests green.

Out of scope

  • The observability enforce category (statically detects Actuator/Micrometer/Quarkus/OTel/Prometheus). The principle's machine-checkable teeth; its own 042 change.
  • The SLO representation (a first-class <spec-slo target="NFR-…"> vs. an NFR annotation) and the verified-tier NFR-quantified rule. Its own spec.
  • The verify-page observables trace giving NFRs a proof leg (extends P-7). Its own spec.
  • New AGENTS.md lines or a numbered FR. Per FR-004 AGENTS points at enforcement commands, not advisory prose; the enforce category will add its "Enforcement floor" line when it lands. FR-001..009 govern the mechanism, unchanged.

3 · Approach

Data/content deltas, the pattern the catalog change established and the new contract now supports. Each delta's target names the manifest content it adds (verified/structured-observability, enterprise/service-level-objectives) and embeds a <spec-diff>, not a <spec-requirement>. Per REQ-CHANGE-002/008, apply recognises these as data deltas: it makes no change to 041's requirements body, folds the §6 tasks, appends the changelog, and archives. The real edit — appending the two principle objects to the verified and enterprise principles[] arrays — is a §6 task drained by /spectastic.implement.

Placement follows the manifest's cumulative-by-repetition model (no cross-profile inheritance): observability is appended to both verified and enterprise; SLOs to enterprise only. The P1a drift-guard test then asserts the two observability copies carry an identical statement. Statements use the manifest's established voice — one or two sentences, imperative, agent-aware, no attribution or internal ids.

4 · Deltas

Two additive data/content deltas. Targets name profile-preset content (data governed by FR-003), embed a <spec-diff>, and — being non-requirement-shaped — leave spec.html's requirements body untouched under the new apply contract.

What's added: one principle appended to the verified preset's principles[] in spectastic-profiles.json, and repeated into the enterprise preset.

Structured observability — The running system emits the golden signals (latency, traffic, errors, saturation) through a standard metrics registry or exporter, so its health is observed in production, not guessed at from logs.
Rationale — tier placement

A production-operation concern: a DORA capability and a production-readiness gate. A team product can start with logs and add this when it operates in production, so it earns its keep at verified. Taxonomy in the survey: Four Golden Signals as the default service-level menu (RED for request services, USE for resources). Its enforce category (Actuator/Micrometer/OTel/Prometheus detection) is deferred — "present ≠ wired" is the honest ceiling there.

What's added: one principle appended to the enterprise preset only.

Service level objectives — Each production-facing reliability target is written as an SLO with an explicit SLI and error budget. The remaining budget, not opinion, sets the pace of change.
Rationale — tier placement

An SLO needs its SLI and instrumentation first, so error budgets sit on the verified observability substrate and land at enterprise. The NFR → SLO → SLI chain and its verify-page proof leg are the deferred artifact/trace specs; here it is the advisory principle only.
