Cards

A spec can be complete and read Draft forever, and nothing notices

Spec upstream

Debugging why ten finished specs still advertised themselves as Draft, root cause was that the flip is a prompt fired at one instant rather than a checkable property of the artifact, so any spec completed outside that instant stays Draft with nothing to detect it; fixed by making the disagreement a finding; regeneration fails, because the requirement describes a moment and a later session has no way to recover it.

Expected
An artifact's status tells the truth about it. A reader, an audit, or the next session can trust the pill without reconstructing completion from git history.
Actual
Ten specs — 016, 017, 018, 063, 099, 100, 101, 102, 103, 104 — carried zero owed tasks and read Draft across all three siblings. Three more were partial bundle flips (REQ-LIFECYCLE-005 forbids these): 001-cli was Superseded with its design and tasks still Draft; 022 and 048 were Accepted with siblings Draft. Every one was corrected by a hand sweep on 17 Aug, which is the only thing that has ever found them.
Diagnosis
REQ-LIFECYCLE-004 hangs the flip on the last tick — the prompt fires when a drain brings the count to zero. That is the right trigger and an incomplete one, because completion arrives by other routes: a spec finished before the prompt existed, a phase retired rather than drained, work landed under another spec's banner, or a drain interrupted between the last tick and the confirmation. In every one of those the count is zero and the moment has passed, and the requirement has nothing to say about the state afterwards. The status is a record of an event where a reader needs a property of the artifact.
Fix
A finding — most likely a folded CLI scan beside the others, since it must read a spec's whole bundle rather than one file — reporting a tasks.html with zero owed tasks whose bundle still reads draft, and a bundle whose three siblings disagree. Report, never auto-flip: REQ-LIFECYCLE-004 is explicit that the command must not flip without confirmation, and a scan that flipped on its own would breach exactly the rule it enforces. The check supplies what the prompt cannot — the disagreement is visible at any time, not only at the instant it appears.
Fixed
Partially closed. Shipped as REQ-LIFECYCLE-009 plus the status-disagreement scan: a finished slice still reading draft warns, a split bundle errors. It found the one live disagreement on its first run — 047, whose spec read accepted while its design and tasks read draft — now fixed. Not closed: the card names three false-backlog mechanisms and this addresses the two that are decidable; the third is tracked as TBD-invariant-criterion-coupling, since an invariant is prose and the coupling is reviewable rather than checkable.
Deep dive — the third of three false-backlog mechanisms, and the only one still unowned

One working session surfaced three ways an artifact can claim something untrue about its own completeness, and they are worth reading together because the third is the one left open.

Superseded phases — a folded phase whose decision a later change reversed stayed as ordinary unticked backlog. Closed by REQ-CHANGE-010; sixteen tasks retired.

Done but unticked — work that landed under another spec's banner, or before the tracker existed. Corrected by the same sweep; no mechanism, and arguably needs none, since the check proposed above would catch its visible symptom.

Complete but Draft — this card. Unowned.

Worth stating plainly: the sweep that found all three ran because an audit had already misread the estate twice in one day. Two of the three were invisible to every check the project has, and the third was invisible to the tooling and to a careful reader, because a Draft pill on a finished spec reads as ordinary work-in-progress rather than as an error. There is no cross-spec contract here and no invariant violated — only a requirement that describes an event where its consumers need a state.
