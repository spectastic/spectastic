1 · Execution strategy

Implement US1 end-to-end — the citation grammar, the form rule, and the 000 widening. That alone makes SC-001's "cites and renders verified" real. Ship, then iterate to US2 (supersession) and US3 (the pack-agnosticism guard) once US1 is verified.

Setup → Foundational → US1 → US2 → US3 → Polish. Each story closes its conformance before the next opens. Recommended here — US2's resolver reads the same grammar Foundational lands, and US3's verb edit is small and self-contained.

One team on each user story after Foundational. Coordinate at the shared seam — the CorpusCitation grammar (§Foundational) — via the spec and plan, not Slack. The US1 propose/apply against 000 should stay with one person; it's a governance action, not a fan-out-friendly implementation task.
