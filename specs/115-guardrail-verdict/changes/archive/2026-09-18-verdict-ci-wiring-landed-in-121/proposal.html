CI wiring landed in 121 — and in the installer, not the example

Applied

1 · Intent

115 designed the verdict for CI — exit 1 on a violation, the changed paths as input, a JSON artifact "so CI can archive it" — and deferred the wiring itself to "the proving example, not the kernel". The audit behind 121-init-ci-gate found the consequence: the verdict had no caller beyond the CLI command, and a downstream project was reliant on a human hand-writing the workflow — the same shape the .claude/commands footgun had before 031.

121 put the wiring in init --tools as a marker-managed, version-pinned, drift-checked file rather than in an example, on the reasoning that an installed guarantee is the only kind that survives (P-8): an example is copied once and never updated. The packaged action form this item imagined — a reusable GitHub Action or GitLab CI component instead of an npx step — is still open, and 121 carries it under its own slug.

2 · Scope

In scope

  • Redirect the TBD-guardrail-verdict-ci out-of-scope item to 121-init-ci-gate, recording the placement change (installer, not example) and pointing the packaged-action remainder at 121's TBD-spectastic-github-action.

Out of scope

  • A packaged GitHub Action / GitLab component — 121's own deferral; not re-planted here. defer-to TBD-spectastic-github-action
  • The gate's behaviour — owned there. defer-to 121-init-ci-gate

3 · Approach

A data/content delta on the out-of-scope block (REQ-CHANGE-002/008); the requirements body is untouched and the §6 task edits the <li>. The rewritten item says the placement changed rather than quietly redirecting, because "belongs to the proving example" was a stated position and a reader deserves to see it reversed, not vanished.
