Widen REQ-LIFECYCLE-006 to accept a corpus source

Applied

1 · Intent

REQ-LIFECYCLE-006 is the grounding requirement: /spectastic.design grounds every design-bearing fact against real source, records it as verified / spike / assumed, and each <spec-decision> cites a verified or spike-resolved source. Its enumeration of what counts as real source is explicitly in-repo: "the actual consuming code, the dependency's signature at the resolved lockfile version, and quantified platform constraints, not the file tree alone."

That enumeration has no place for a domain fact — a settlement window, a regulatory citation, a clinical threshold. So today a domain claim can only ever be recorded assumed, and renders as a visible failure, no matter how carefully the author checked it, because there was nothing in-repo to cite. Spec 051 committed the missing thing: a knowledge corpus of citable, edition-pinned documents. This proposal widens REQ-LIFECYCLE-006's paragraph-1 enumeration of verified sources to include one — a corpus document cited by its edition-pinned id — so a domain fact can finally be verified against something a reviewer can open.

The change is deliberately minimal and additive: it adds one guarded item to a single enumeration. Every existing grounding source stays valid and un-migrated, and paragraphs 2–4 are the live text verbatim. The concrete citation grammar (KB-NNN@edition), its corpus-citation-form rule, its resolve/staleness gates, and the "MAY cite" verb binding are all 052's (and 053's) scope, not this proposal's — deliberately not transcribed into the meta-spec, so the grammar has exactly one owner. This is only the contract widening 052's FR-001 depends on.

2 · Scope

In scope

  • MODIFY REQ-LIFECYCLE-006 — add one guarded item to paragraph 1's enumerated real-source list: "where a knowledge corpus is present, a corpus document cited by its edition-pinned id". Paragraphs 2–4 stay verbatim; the concrete grammar is not transcribed (it stays 052's).

Out of scope

  • The KB-NNN@edition citation grammar, the corpus-citation-form rule, the resolve/staleness gates, and the "MAY cite" verb binding — all 052's (and 053's) scope. defer-to=052-corpus-citation-contract.
  • Making corpus grounding a MUST or a hard gate — grounding stays a SHOULD discipline (undecidable whether a claim is a domain claim); unchanged by this proposal. defer-to=never.
  • Any change to the grounding gate (the MUST NOT ready-for-tasks clause) — untouched. defer-to=never.

3 · Approach

A surgical widening of paragraph 1's source enumeration — one guarded item, gated on a corpus being present so a corpus-less project reads exactly as before. Paragraphs 2, 3, and 4 are the live text byte-for-byte (the adversarial pass caught an earlier draft that also rewrote paragraph 2 and added a rationale sentence to paragraph 4 — both reverted). The concrete grammar (KB-NNN@edition) is deliberately not transcribed: naming the token here would duplicate 052's FR-002 and drift from it the moment 052's still-Draft form shifts, so the requirement names the kind of source ("cited by its edition-pinned id") and leaves the grammar to its single owner.

4 · Deltas

One <spec-delta> per requirement touched. target= links to the live spec. op= is one of added | modified | removed | renamed; a missing or invalid op renders visibly broken.

What changes: paragraph 1's enumerated real-source list gains one guarded item — "where a knowledge corpus is present, a corpus document cited by its edition-pinned id". Nothing else moves: paragraphs 2–4 are byte-for-byte the live text, the concrete citation grammar stays single-sourced in 052 (not transcribed here), and the SHOULD-discipline / MUST-gate split is unchanged.

The /spectastic.design procedure SHOULD ground every design-bearing fact against real source before its interview — opening the actual consuming code, the dependency's signature at the resolved lockfile version, quantified platform constraints, and — where a knowledge corpus is present — a corpus document cited by its edition-pinned id, not the file tree alone — and SHOULD record each fact in an evidence ledger as one of verified (opened this turn, with a cited source), spike (resolved by a time-boxed investigation run during planning, with its finding), or assumed (unverified).

Each <spec-decision> SHOULD declare its grounding and cite a verified or spike-resolved source in its Context; a decision that rests on an unverified assumption SHOULD render as a visible failure (the planning analogue of REQ-AUTHOR-001's render-broken-when-malformed discipline).

The gate is the binding clause: the plan MUST NOT be declared ready for /spectastic.tasks while a must-tier decision rests on an assumed or unresolved spike fact, unless the author has accepted it as a tracked <spec-risk> whose status the author commits (per REQ-CHANGE-004's author-owned-status model). This gate is a design→tasks readiness affirmation, not a Draft content-lock — it mirrors the existing estimability gate, and pure-judgment decisions with no external fact are exempt.

Implementation surprises come from planning under unverified assumption. The grounding discipline ships as SHOULD — mirroring REQ-CHANGE-004, which shipped the adversarial pass as guidance from one data point rather than a blanket MUST that hollows into theater. The gate is the MUST that actually stops an ungrounded assumption from reaching implementation silently; lifting the /spectastic.explain "cite only confirmed source" discipline into planning makes "what the plan knows vs. assumes" structurally legible.

5 · Risk register

Populated by the adversarial risk pass (this proposal touches a must-tier requirement). One <spec-risk> per finding. target= is required; status= defaults to identified and apply refuses until each transitions to accepted, mitigated, or rejected.

The original draft transcribed the literal KB-NNN@edition grammar token into the must-tier meta-spec, while 052 FR-002 — still Draft — is that grammar's authoritative owner. Two artifacts would own one grammar; the moment 052's form shifts during its own build, the frozen meta-spec text goes stale and contradicts its downstream owner — the exact drift the 051→052→053 split exists to prevent.

Original post-state: "a corpus document cited as KB-NNN@edition" inlined into REQ-LIFECYCLE-006.

Mitigated by construction: the delta now names the kind of source ("a corpus document cited by its edition-pinned id") and does not transcribe the grammar token. KB-NNN@edition stays single-sourced in 052's FR-002; the meta-spec references the concept, not the syntax, so a grammar change can't stale this requirement.

The widening opens a verified path for domain facts before the gate that checks such a citation exists — the resolve/staleness rules are deferred to 053 (unbuilt). In the window between this apply and 053, a decision could cite a fabricated or dangling corpus id, render quiet-green verified, and clear the MUST-NOT-ready gate with nothing checking the id resolves.

REQ-LIFECYCLE-006's verified definition ("opened this turn, with a cited source") + the MUST-NOT-ready gate, against 052/053's deferred resolve rule.

Accepted and recorded, not hidden (the P-8/P-9 posture). The gap is real but bounded and no worse than the status quo: in-repo verified sources (src/foo.ts:88) are already author-asserted — nothing checks they resolve either; verified has always been an honesty affirmation, not a machine proof. 052 ships the corpus-citation-form warn (form well-formedness) immediately; 053's provenance gate (error on an unresolvable id) is the named closer, which will make corpus citations better-checked than in-repo ones have ever been. The window is the honest cost of shipping the contract before its gate.

The original delta was broader than its single in-scope bullet: beyond paragraph 1's list it inserted a new appositive enumeration into paragraph 2 (with no "when a corpus is present" guard, so a corpus-less project would read an unconditional corpus option) and a rationale sentence into paragraph 4 — contradicting the Approach's "reads exactly as before" promise and 051's graceful-absence NFR.

§2 In-scope named only "the enumerated real-source list" (paragraph 1), but the post-state also rewrote paragraphs 2 and 4.

Mitigated by narrowing the delta to paragraph 1 only — one guarded item ("where a knowledge corpus is present"), paragraphs 2–4 reverted to the live text byte-for-byte. §2 and §3 now describe exactly that, and the graceful-absence promise (a corpus-less project reads unchanged) holds.
