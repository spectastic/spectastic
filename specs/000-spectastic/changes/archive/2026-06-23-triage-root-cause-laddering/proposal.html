Triage root-cause laddering

Applied

1 · Intent

Triage's format is deliberately fast (a card reads in under 30 seconds), and its strongest discipline — "fix at the highest layer that needed to change" — was stated as a maxim with no step that enforces it. The result is a speed bias toward the layer the symptom appears in: the 020 T-005 defect (a needs-attention border) was first classified at the presentation layer and only reached its true cross-spec owner (the <spec-questions> format contract) after the author pushed back.

The fix is to make the existing regeneration test bidirectional: it already answers "would another session reproduce this?" for one layer; the ladder applies it as a climb — step up while a fix would still recur — and stops at the lowest layer that holds. That lowest-passing rung is the real owner: high enough that the bug won't recur (the T-005 miss), no higher, so honest drift isn't inflated past its layer (the No spec inflation discipline).

2 · Scope

In scope

  • ADD REQ-LIFECYCLE-007 — the root-cause-laddering discipline (SHOULD) + the bidirectional classification gate (MUST: classify at the lowest layer whose fix passes the regeneration test).
  • Wire the ladder into commands/spectastic.triage.md: the regeneration-test step, the Classify step, and the "fix at the highest layer" discipline line.

Out of scope

  • A second, heavier analysis pass — the ladder is one probe wired into the existing regeneration test, to preserve the <30-second-card speed.
  • just-do / defer intake items — they skip the regeneration test by design, so the ladder does not apply to them.

3 · Approach

No new mechanism — the ladder is the regeneration test, climbed. Step 5 (regeneration test) and step 6 (Classify the layer) of commands/spectastic.triage.md gain a single probe between them: "if I changed only this candidate layer, would the regeneration test still fail? If so, the owner is upstream — step up (implementation → spec → cross-spec → principles) and re-ask, stopping at the lowest layer that holds." The standing "fix at the highest layer that needed to change" and "No spec inflation" discipline lines are reconciled as the two bounds of one gate — climb until it won't recur, stop at the lowest layer that holds.

Mirrors how REQ-LIFECYCLE-006 (grounded planning) and REQ-CHANGE-004 (the adversarial pass) lifted a command discipline into a meta-spec requirement — and copies their split exactly: the laddering discipline ships SHOULD (codifying from one data point), and the MUST is reserved for the bidirectional gate (lowest passing layer), which binds both under-classification and spec inflation.

4 · Deltas

One <spec-delta> — ADD REQ-LIFECYCLE-007. Post-state rendered inline.

Before committing a diagnostic layer, /spectastic.triage SHOULD ladder the root cause against the regeneration test: starting from the candidate (lowest plausible) layer, ask "if I changed only this layer, would the regeneration test still fail?" — if the bug would recur, the owner is upstream, so step up (implementation → spec → cross-spec → principles) and re-ask. The ladder reuses the existing regeneration test — one probe, not a second analysis pass — so the card still reads in under 30 seconds.

The gate is the binding clause: a card MUST be classified at the lowest layer whose fix makes the regeneration test pass — neither lower (a fix that would recur — the symptom layer) nor higher (an honest implementation drift inflated past its real owner, against the No spec inflation discipline). That lowest-passing layer is "the highest layer that needed to change," stated as a procedure: the gate is bidirectional, so the ladder is not a one-way escalator. just-do and defer intake items skip the regeneration test and are exempt.

Rationale

The maxim had no step, so triage defaulted to the convenient layer: the no-open-questions defect (020 T-005) was classed at the presentation layer and reached cross-spec only on author pushback. The laddering discipline ships SHOULD — codifying from one data point, mirroring REQ-LIFECYCLE-006 / REQ-CHANGE-004 rather than a blanket MUST that hollows into theater. The gate is the MUST, and it is bidirectional: it stops under-classification (T-005) and over-escalation (spec inflation) with the same rule — stop at the lowest layer that holds.
