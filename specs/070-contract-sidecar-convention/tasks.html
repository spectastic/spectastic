20 tasks across five phases. This slice is the cohort's prerequisite — Foundational lands the shared readContractDeclarations() that 071–074 all import, so it must complete before those specs start. Tests for a story MUST be written and failing before its implementation tasks start.

1 · Execution strategy

Foundational plus US1 is the whole value: a declared path that resolves to nothing is caught. US2 and US3 are the structural exclusion and the documented convention, and can follow.

Setup → Foundational → US1 → US2 → US3 → Polish. Recommended. US1 and US2 both touch contractResolveFindings(), so running them in sequence avoids a collision in one function.

Poor fit within this slice — it is one reader, one finding producer and one scan. The parallelism this cohort has is between specs, and it unlocks only once this one is done.
