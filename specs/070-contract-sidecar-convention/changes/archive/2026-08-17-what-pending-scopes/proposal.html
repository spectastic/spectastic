What pending scopes

A term defined to decide what a checker reports was borrowed to decide what a reader sees.

Status Applied Change ID 2026-08-17-what-pending-scopes Spec 070-contract-sidecar-convention Author Brian Corbin · @briancorbinxyz Created 17 Aug 2026 Source 072/T-002 Targets 1 requirement — 1 modified Read time

FR-008 defines pending promotion as the effective path not resolving. That boundary is exactly right for what it was written to decide — whether validate reports a missing contract — and wrong for anything else, because it excludes every amendment after the first, where both copies are readable.

072 borrowed the term to decide which copy a view projects, and inherited that exclusion: a reviewer reading a design that proposed changing an existing interface would have seen the old contract. Caught before it shipped. This says what the term scopes, so the next borrower does not have to be caught.

1 · Intent

The term is doing two jobs and only earned one of them. Pending promotion was defined to answer a reporting question — is an unresolved effective path a defect, or the normal state of a design mid-flight? For that, "the effective path does not resolve" is precisely the right line, because a contract that does resolve needs no exemption from a check about missing files.

Read as a general description of a contract's lifecycle it says something false. A contract being amended is in flight in every sense a reader means — a proposal exists, promotion has not run, the shape is under discussion — and it is not pending under FR-008, because the previous version still resolves. The word suggests a phase; the definition names a symptom.

Nothing is wrong with FR-008. What is missing is the sentence that stops it being read as more than it is.

In scope

Out of scope

4 · Deltas

What changes — the rationale only. The normative text is untouched, word for word, and so are all three existing rationale paragraphs; one paragraph is added after them.

A design whose declared effective contract does not resolve, while the proposed location holds a contract of that name, is pending promotion. A pending contract MUST NOT satisfy any check that requires an effective contract.

Rationale

The state is the normal condition of every design between authoring and promotion, so erroring on it would fire on all of them — and today it does, which is why a real design run could not validate at all.

The remaining clause is what keeps FR-003 exactly: a pending contract is unreported and uncounted, which are different things. It still cannot mark an enforcement category covered or stand in for an effective declaration.

Both locations are derived rather than declared, following the convention promotion already uses. The recorded ceiling: a design that is never promoted stays silent forever, and this makes that legal. Deliberate — the alternative reports every in-flight design to catch the few that stall — but nothing then detects an abandoned contract, and the honest place to say so is here.

Added by this change — what this boundary was drawn for. The condition above answers a reporting question: is an unresolved effective path a defect, or the normal state of a design in flight? For that, keying on the declared path not resolving is exactly right, because a contract that does resolve needs no exemption from a check about missing files. It is not a name for a phase of a contract's life, and reading it as one gets the common case backwards — a contract being amended in place is in flight in every sense a reader means, yet does not meet this condition, because the version being replaced still resolves at the declared path.

Two things that paragraph is deliberately careful about, because a first draft of it got both wrong. It says in place: an amendment that also relocates the contract declares a path that does not resolve while the sidecar holds the file, so it does meet this condition and is pending — the ordinary case once detection widens to ecosystem-conventional paths. And it does not claim the term's reach ends at validate: the clause above binds any check requiring an effective contract, which FR-003 makes concrete by naming the enforcement tally — a consumer that is not validate at all.

This is rationale rather than a rule, and that is the point rather than a compromise. A sentence about how to read a requirement has no defect condition: no artifact, fixture or run can breach it, so wrapping it in a conformance keyword would add must-tier coverage that nothing can cover — the pretence of a check that P-8 excludes. An earlier draft of this very change did exactly that, hours after the same mistake was corrected elsewhere in the estate.

5 · Risk register

"A contract being amended is not pending" is a universal that is false whenever the amendment relocates the contract

FR-008's condition is about the declared path resolving, not about a previous version existing somewhere. An amendment that also moves the contract declares a new path that does not resolve while the sidecar holds the file — so it meets the condition word for word and the scan treats it as pending, while my sentence said it is not. The ordinary case once detection widens to ecosystem-conventional paths, and exactly what 076's key-stability change records as silently re-keying.

Narrowed to "in place", with the relocation case named explicitly. The regret the finding describes is real and specific: this sentence exists to be quoted by the next borrower choosing a predicate, so a false universal in it does more damage than the ambiguity it was written to remove.

"This term scopes what validate reports" contradicts a rationale paragraph shipping verbatim above it

The clause immediately preceding says a pending contract must not satisfy any check requiring an effective contract, and FR-003 makes that concrete by naming the enforcement tally — a consumer that is not validate. FR-008's own carried rationale says it outright: "unreported and uncounted, which are different things". A reader taking the new sentence at face value would let the enforcement floor count a pending contract, which is what FR-003 exists to forbid.

Removed; the rationale now says the boundary was drawn for the reporting question without claiming the term's reach ends there. Worth stating plainly: I carried that rationale verbatim — correctly, and with the guard confirming it — and then wrote a sentence contradicting it two lines below. Carrying text is not the same as reading it.

The deferral rested on a count that was wrong: four consumers exist, not one

It deferred naming the predicate because "one instance is not evidence a vocabulary item earns its keep". It is must-tier in 072 FR-009 and independently re-derived in three code paths — sidecarPathsFor, an inline copy in the materialiser that does not use that helper, and the view-stale compare target. So this proposal spent its delta clarifying the predicate 072 abandoned, while the one it now rests on has no definition anywhere.

Still deferred, on a different and honest reason. The predicate is ambiguous where it is load-bearing — resolution is by basename, and two versioned contracts collide on one proposed path — so naming it before that is settled would fix the wrong half. The deferral now records the four consumers and points at the slug that owns the ambiguity, rather than resting on a miscount.

The inline copy in the materialiser is a real duplication of a helper whose own comment says it exists so the check and the verb cannot drift apart. Worth its own card.

A MUST NOT governing an act of reading has no defect condition

KB-0001@2026-07-25 commits that a MUST is an absolute requirement whose absence is a defect. "MUST NOT be read as naming a phase" governs a reader, so no artifact, fixture or run can breach it — yet it enters the conformance index beside genuinely checkable clauses and counts as must-tier coverage nothing can cover. P-8 names the shape: the strongest available check, never a pretence of one.

The delta is now rationale-only. The normative text is untouched and the guidance lives where it can be said carefully without conformance weight — which also dissolved the other two findings, since both were defects in sentences that should never have been rules.

This is the mistake I explicitly asked the pass to look for, having made it on 044 this morning, and then made anyway in the same session. It is the third distinct occasion today where knowing a failure was not sufficient to avoid it, which is the argument for the pass being a standing control rather than a courtesy.
