Retract the consumer-only stance

063 made a corpus discoverable by default, so 062's two consumer-only clauses — no root marketplace.json, concepts never listed — are now false; record their supersession.

Status Applied Change ID 2026-07-27-retract-consumer-only Spec 062-corpus-identity-migration Author Brian Corbin · @briancorbin Created 27 Jul 2026 Targets 2 requirements (modified) Read time

062 recorded the meta-repo's corpus as consumer-only — FR-009 forbade a distributable root marketplace.json, and FR-002 forbade listing spectastic-concepts in one. 063-corpus-discoverability retracted that stance: a corpus is discoverable by default, and the meta-repo now carries a root knowledge/marketplace.json listing both packs (concepts self-declared tool-specific, spared by 057's now-amended rule). Both clauses are false as written. MODIFY FR-002 and FR-009 to record the supersession — the sibling doc-sync to the 057 amendment that just landed.

Retract the consumer-only stance

Applied

1 · Intent

062 hit the pack-not-portable collision (a discoverable corpus can't list the tool's own pack) and retreated to consumer-only, deferring publication to TBD-corpus-publish. 063 owned that TBD and resolved the collision instead — a self-declared tool-specific exemption (057, amended by the sibling propose that just landed) — then made a corpus discoverable by default and dogfooded it: the meta-repo's knowledge/marketplace.json is now committed, listing both packs.

That leaves two 062 clauses factually false: FR-002's "MUST NOT be declared in a distributable marketplace.json" and FR-009's "MUST NOT carry a distributable root marketplace.json". This proposal records their supersession so 062's own text stops asserting the opposite of what shipped — never a silent contradiction (P-6).

2 · Scope

In scope

  • MODIFY FR-002 — drop the no-marketplace clause; concepts is now listed, tool-specific.
  • MODIFY FR-009 — record consumer-only as superseded by 063 FR-007.

Out of scope

  • The 057 pack-not-portable amendment — its own propose, applied first (this proposal depends on it, already landed).
  • 062's §1 Context / §4 data-model prose that mentions consumer-only in passing — it reads as the historical narrative of that slice and stays accurate as history; only the two live requirements are corrected.
  • Any code change — 063 already built and committed everything; this is prose-only.

3 · Approach

Two MODIFYs, both preserving the requirement id (P-3) and keeping the true half of each requirement. FR-002 keeps "filed under the spectastic namespace" and retracts only the marketplace prohibition; FR-009 keeps the record that consumer-only was the 062-slice decision and marks it superseded. Each new rationale drops the now-obsolete "057 D-002 keeps un-listed packs exempt by construction" reasoning — that framing was overturned when 063/057 replaced exempt-by-omission with an explicit self-declaration.

4 · Deltas

Two <spec-delta>.

What changes: the "MUST NOT be declared in a distributable marketplace.json" clause is retracted — concepts is now listed there as a self-declared tool-specific pack.

The spectastic-concepts pack MUST be filed in the registry under the project-local namespace spectastic (supplied by the import coordinate spectastic-concepts@spectastic). 062's original companion clause — that it must not be declared in a distributable knowledge/marketplace.json — is superseded by 063-corpus-discoverability: concepts is now listed in the root knowledge/marketplace.json and self-declares tool-specific: true in its SKILL.md, which 057's amended pack-not-portable rule spares.

Rationale

The pack is spectastic's own dogfood corpus — it legitimately carries <spec-*> tags, verb names, and the word "spectastic". 062 kept it un-listed because 057's rule then had no vocabulary for an intentionally tool-specific pack (exempt-by-omission). 063 gave the rule that vocabulary — an explicit self-declaration — so the pack can be both discoverable AND honestly tool-specific. The namespace label and the tool-specific flag together carry the identity; the marketplace listing carries the discoverability.

What changes: consumer-only is retracted — recorded as the 062-slice decision now superseded by 063's discoverable-by-default.

062's migration slice recorded the meta-repo's corpus role as consumer-only — its knowledge/ read from and dogfooded, carrying no distributable root marketplace.json. That stance is superseded by 063-corpus-discoverability (FR-007): a corpus is discoverable by default, and the meta-repo now carries a root knowledge/marketplace.json listing spectastic-concepts (tool-specific) and finance-settlement. This requirement is retained as the historical record of the 062-slice decision and the reasoning that produced it — not as a live constraint.

Rationale

Surfaced by triage T-001: 062 fixed the consumer role but left the publisher role undecided and deferred it to TBD-corpus-publish. 063 owned that TBD, resolved the pack-not-portable collision that forced the deferral, and reversed the default — so the "does not publish in this slice" clause is now spent. Keeping the id and marking it superseded preserves the why (P-3), rather than erasing the decision.

5 · Risk register

Adversarial pass ran (touches two must-tier requirements). All findings dispositioned by the author.

The change most likely to be regretted: reframing a spent MUST NOT as "superseded, retained as history" rather than REMOVE-ing it leaves a must-tier requirement in 062 that a naive reader could still treat as binding.

MODIFY FR-002 / FR-009: "superseded by 063 … retained as the historical record".

Accepted. REMOVE would erase why consumer-only ever happened (the collision, the triage, the deferral) — exactly the provenance P-3 says IDs preserve. The word "superseded" plus the direct link to 063's live requirement makes the status unambiguous; a reader who follows the link lands on what's actually in force. A dangling REMOVE-with-migration would carry less of that history.

The requirement most likely contradicted is 062's own FR-002 rationale as it stood — it justified un-listing by citing "057 D-002 keeps un-listed packs exempt by construction, not an exemption list". The new state IS an exemption list (the self-declaration), so leaving that reasoning would be internally contradictory.

The old FR-002 rationale's "by construction, not by an exemption list" clause.

Mitigated. Both deltas' rationales are rewritten to drop that obsolete framing and state the new one explicitly — 063 replaced exempt-by-omission with an explicit self-declaration. The 057 amendment (applied first) already made 057's own text consistent with this, so the cross-spec triple (057/062/063) now agrees.

Scope concern: the proposal deliberately leaves 062's §1/§4 prose that mentions consumer-only untouched — if a reader takes that narrative as current rather than historical, the correction feels half-done.

§2 Out of scope: "062's §1 Context / §4 data-model prose … stays accurate as history".

Accepted. The §1/§4 mentions are past-tense narrative of what the 062 slice did, which stays true — 062 did go consumer-only. Only the two forward-looking MUST/MUST NOT requirements assert a live constraint, and those are exactly what's corrected. Rewriting the historical narrative would falsify the record of the slice.

No corpus domain-fact contradiction: the repo's knowledge/ corpus commits SDD/RFC 2119 and securities-settlement facts, orthogonal to a corpus-publication-policy reversal.
